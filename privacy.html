Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application and services, including our integration with TikTok.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our service or log in via TikTok, we may collect the following information:

  • TikTok Profile Information: Basic profile information (such as display name, profile photo, and open ID) provided through TikTok Login Kit with your explicit authorization.
  • User-Generated Content: Media files (photos and videos), captions, and post metadata that you upload or create using our platform.
  • Usage Data: Technical logs, device type, and app interactions to ensure normal operation and platform performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our social media management and automation services.
  • To allow you to log in, sync media, and publish content directly to your TikTok account.
  • To assist in generating AI-driven captions, tags, and media schedules based on your input.
  • To improve user experience and ensure platform security.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share data with third-party service providers (such as TikTok API and cloud storage providers) strictly necessary to deliver our application's functionalities.

4. Data Retention and Security

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. We implement standard security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You have the right to access, modify, or request the deletion of your data at any time. You can revoke our application's access to your TikTok account directly through your TikTok Security & App Permissions settings. To request full deletion of your account data stored on our servers, please contact us.
